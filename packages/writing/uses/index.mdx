## Software and workflow

### Editors and AI tools

- [Zed](https://zed.dev/) and [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) are my editors.
- [T3 Code](https://t3.codes/) and [Conductor](https://conductor.build/) are the AI coding tools I use.
- [Dia](https://www.diabrowser.com/) is my browser, and [Hex](https://hex.kitlangton.com/) by Kit Langton is my dictation tool.

### Terminal and local development

- [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) is my terminal emulator.
- I use [zsh](https://www.zsh.org/) with [Oh My Zsh](https://ohmyz.sh/) for shell configuration.
- [OrbStack](https://orbstack.dev/) provides a lightweight local environment for containers and Linux machines on macOS.
- [`btop`](https://github.com/aristocratos/btop), [`tree`](https://github.com/Old-Man-Programmer/tree), and [`lsd`](https://github.com/lsd-rs/lsd) help me inspect processes and files.

## Building software

### Languages and frameworks

My work often spans [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) and [JavaScript](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript), with [React](https://react.dev/), [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/), and [TanStack Start](https://tanstack.com/start/latest) for web applications. I also build mobile apps with [Expo](https://expo.dev/) and [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/), and have used [Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/).

### Workspaces and monorepos

For smaller projects, I reach for [Bun](https://bun.sh/) and [Bun workspaces](https://bun.sh/docs/install/workspaces). For larger projects, I use [pnpm workspaces](https://pnpm.io/workspaces) with [Turborepo](https://turborepo.com/) to share packages and coordinate builds.

### Backend services, data, and email

- [BullMQ](https://bullmq.io/) for background jobs and queues.
- [Elysia](https://elysiajs.com/) and [Hono](https://hono.dev/) for backend services and APIs.
- [Convex](https://www.convex.dev/), [Neon](https://neon.tech/), and [PlanetScale](https://planetscale.com/) for database needs, depending on the project.
- [Resend](https://resend.com/) for sending email.

### Analytics and hosting

- I use [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) or [Umami](https://umami.is/) for analytics, depending on the project.
- I deploy to [Vercel](https://vercel.com/), [Render](https://render.com/), or [Railway](https://railway.app/). On Render, I typically deploy Dockerized applications.

### API and debugging tools

- [Hoppscotch](https://hoppscotch.io/) for exploring and testing HTTP APIs.
- [Browser developer tools](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/devtools) and application logs help me reproduce interface and integration issues.
- [Git](https://git-scm.com/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/) for source control, reviews, and project history.

## How I choose tools

I prefer tools that stay out of the way, work well together, and make it easy to understand what is happening when something breaks. I will try a new tool when it solves a real friction point, but I avoid adding dependencies or workflow steps just because they are popular. This list is a practical snapshot, and it changes as my setup and projects change.
